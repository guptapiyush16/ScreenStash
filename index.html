YOUR STUFF STAYS YOURS

A little more helpful.
A lot less creepy.

Screenshots can be personal. ScreenStash is being designed around local-first processing, so your private moments don't need to leave your computer to become useful.

✓
Local-first processing
Designed to use on-device AI and OCR. No external servers receive your captures.
✓
Your files stay in your control
Keep originals in your existing OS screenshots folder. SQLite stays on your hard drive.
✓
No account needed for MVP
Your personal stash doesn't need a cloud login, email verification, or tracking.
